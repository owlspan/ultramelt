ULTRAMELT 2: MONOPOLY
=====================
You took the South Pole. Now build the plant. Mine, chop, craft, belt it
into machines, fill head office's contracts, and buy up the land.

FILES
  ultramelt2.exe       Windows
  ultramelt2-linux     Linux   (chmod +x ultramelt2-linux, then ./ultramelt2-linux)
  ultramelt2-macos     Mac     (chmod +x, and if macOS blocks it:
                                xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ultramelt2-macos)
  server/              the dedicated server (no graphics needed): see SERVER.txt
  source/              the whole game's source; ./build.sh, ./dist.sh

PRESS G IN THE GAME FOR THE GUIDE. The task at the top of the screen walks
you through the first steps (mining, conveyors, storage, smelting).

NEW IN v35: 12 PLAYERS, LANGUAGES, A HELPER
  - Up to 12 players at once (it was 4, so a dedicated server only fit 3).
    server.properties: max-players=11 (not counting the server). Old
    server.properties files without it get 11.
  - HOST A GAME has SERVER SETTINGS: own companies or all together, most
    companies, fighting between/within companies, passwords, most players.
    Saved as server.properties next to your worlds (same names as a
    dedicated server's, so you can copy it to Wispbyte).
  - The helper: hold a machine and a box says how to hook it up, with OUT
    (where things come out) and IN marked on the ground. Look at a placed
    machine and it shows what it needs, what it has, and what's wrong
    (no coal, nothing at OUT, full, no spin).
  - Workbench search: click SEARCH at the top right and type. Finds tools,
    machines and materials from every tab.
  - The markets screen always lists what sells in each market (SELL TO
    STAY IN), even after you're in it.
  - Languages: Settings > LANGUAGE: English, Espanol, Portugues, Francais.
  - Ice walls (and anything that comes down) shatter where they are
    instead of sinking through what's below.
  - The white window on start: if the start hangs it restarts itself; after
    two failed starts in a row it starts without the graphics card once.
    The log (ultramelt2_log.txt) now says which step it was on.

NEW IN v34: THE AUTO COOK, AND TIDYING YOUR STUFF
  - AUTO COOK (workbench, machines tab: 6 plate, 2 gear, 4 coal): a brick
    oven for your factory. Belt raw meat, raw fish or dirty water in and
    steak, cooked fish or clean water come out the front. It switches to
    whatever it's fed once it's empty.
  - Picking up a campfire with food on it gives you the food back (cooked
    if it was done, raw if not) instead of it vanishing.
  - Inventory and lockers: point at a slot and press Q to drop one,
    CTRL+Q to drop the whole stack.
  - BURN IT on the inventory screen: pick something up, click BURN IT,
    click again to be sure. It's gone for good.

NEW IN v33: LOCKERS WITHOUT DUPLICATES (OR DELAY)
  - Clicking in a locker happens on your screen at once: no waiting on
    the server after each click.
  - The server checks every click against the real locker. If it doesn't
    match (someone else took it first, or clicks crossed), it refuses, and
    your game undoes that click and the ones after it: your cursor and
    inventory go back to what you really have. Moving things around fast
    can't make copies any more.

NEW IN v31: COMPANIES, ROGUES AND GANGS (servers with companies=separate)
  - Joining: you choose. Join a company that's there, found your own (you
    name it) while there's room (max-companies), or go rogue.
  - U: your company screen. Founders rename their company, fire people
    and let people in. Anyone else can leave (and goes rogue).
  - ROGUES have no company: no land, can't buy or sell at a head office,
    can only build a campfire, water pot or beacon (made by hand on the U
    screen), get hungry and thirsty faster, and wake up out in the wild.
    But they're quicker on foot, their name only shows up close, raids
    leave them alone, the hospital only bills $100 + 10%, the trader
    fences their stuff (40%), and killing a company player takes 35% of
    that player's hospital bill. Ask to join a company, found one, or...
  - GANGS: a rogue for 2 days can start a gang and let other rogues in.
    A shared purse, a camp (your campfire is where you wake up), and camp
    things to build anywhere free: walls, doors, beds, lamps, lockers,
    floors, roofs. Still no land, no machines, no head office.
  - Fighting players: server.properties pvp-companies=true (different
    companies, rogues and gangs can hurt each other) and pvp-team=false
    (your own team can't). Guns do half damage to people (55 at most).

NEW IN v30: HOSPITAL BILLS
  - Dying costs money now: the hospital bills $500 plus a quarter of the
    company's cash. Paid in full, you wake up with food and water at 60.
  - Can't cover it? They take everything you have and patch you up
    cheaply: you wake up hungry and thirsty (20). Dying is no longer a
    free way to refill food and water.

NEW IN v29: REAL CAVES, AND GOING DOWN
  - Cave mouths are rocky hills with a dark arch you can see from far off.
    Walk into the arch to go in; the hill round it is solid rock.
  - Inside, caves are much bigger: wide halls, open chambers, a high roof,
    rough rock walls, icicles and stalagmites.
  - Glowing chalk arrows on the floor show the way back out, and an arrow
    at the bottom of the screen says how far it is (in mines too).
  - When someone dies, everyone sees it: their knees give, they topple
    over, and they stay down until they wake up again.

NEW IN v28: THE MARKETS (L)
  - Everything you sell belongs to a market: FUEL, ELECTRONICS, VEHICLES,
    CONSTRUCTION, LUXURY, FOOD, HIGH TECH. The first time you sell into
    one, you enter it - and meet the rival firms already there (Glacier
    Oil, Snowchip, Tundra Motors...). L shows them all.
  - MARKET SHARE follows what you sell, against what everyone else
    pushes. 40%+ and the biggest share: you DOMINATE that market. The
    board now also wants 4 dominated markets before the takeover.
  - YOUR PRICE in each market: CHEAP (-20%, wins share fast), NORMAL, or
    PREMIUM (+25%, loses share).
  - RIVALS FIGHT BACK: price wars on whoever's winning (your prices
    there drop 15%), price changes, and newcomers moving in cheap.
    The Polar Times reports it all.
  - PRICE PACTS: agree prices with a rival (or another player's company)
    - no price wars between you, and +15% on everything you sell there.
    Rivals won't deal with you till you hold 10%, or while they're angry.
    Going CHEAP breaks every pact in that market - and they declare war.
  - SQUEEZE: with a pact partner, gang up on someone (newcomers most of
    all) and push them out of the market.
  - BUY OUT a rival once you hold 15%: its whole share becomes yours.
  - OWN COMPANIES games: players' companies compete in the markets too,
    can offer each other pacts and squeezes (accept them in L).
  - FIXED: mine carts in caves used the wrong model since v26, and two
    timers (scaffold wear, worn-gear sync) were tangled since v19.

NEW IN v27: AUTOMATIC UPDATES
  - The game checks github.com/owlspan/ultramelt (branch ultramelt2)
    when it starts. If there's a newer version, click UPDATE at the top
    of the title screen: it downloads, installs and restarts by itself.
    The files there are scrambled - only the game can open them, and
    it checks each one before using it. update.txt: url=off turns it off.
    (UltraMelt 1 updates the same way, from branch ultramelt.)

NEW IN v26: ELECTRICITY, RAIL CARTS
  - ELECTRICITY: machines (miners, smelters, assemblers, everything that
    makes things) now run at 70% speed without power and at 160% with
    it. A running GENERATOR powers 6 tiles round it. POWER POLES
    (LOGISTICS tab, 3 wood + 2 wire) carry it on: a pole within 6 tiles
    of a running generator, or 10 of another live pole, lights up and
    powers 4 tiles round it. Live poles glow.
  - RAIL STATIONS (LOGISTICS tab): belt items into a station; a cart
    carries up to 10 at a time to your nearest other station on the
    same channel (E changes it, 1-9) - across plots that don't touch,
    over water, anywhere. Items come out of the far station's front.
    The way to move goods between bases now that storage + inserter
    is slow.

NEW IN v25: THE TRADER, RAIDS
  - THE TRADER: on even days a caravan camps by head office from morning
    until mid-afternoon (E on the trader). It sells three rare parts and

  - RAIDS: from day 10, every third night a pack comes for one of your

    bear that used to now waits for day 10 too.
  - FIXED (v24): E on a waypoint beacon opened the boardroom.

NEW IN v17: NO MORE FREEZING WHEN HOSTING, MAC FIXES, MORE DETAIL
  - HOSTING NO LONGER SLOWS DOWN AND FREEZES. While a game was hosted (or
    joined), memory was never cleaned up: it grew by tens of megabytes a
    second until the game stuttered more and more and froze. The network
    is now read without waiting threads, so memory stays flat. Tested:
    a host and a friend for six minutes, memory steady on both.
  - If the game closed straight away after you force-quit it: an old copy
    may still be running. End every ultramelt2.exe in Task Manager (or
    restart), then start it again.
  - MAC: every key types (the right half of the keyboard did nothing), no
    more alert sound on each key press, the mouse turns the camera, and
    the online code is drawn smaller.
  - FARTHER DETAIL: ore, coal and crystals stay visible twice as far,
    trees keep their full shape much further out, the hills are more
    detailed in the distance, and buildings are drawn until the fog
    hides them. VIEW DISTANCE in settings goes further too.
  - 5 SECONDS OF PROTECTION after you wake up: nothing can hurt you
    ("PROTECTED" blinks under the crosshair).
  - SWIMMING in the Leviathan's flooded lair: hold W and you swim where
    you look (look up to rise, down to dive), SPACE swims up, CTRL/C
    swims down, SHIFT swims faster. You hang in the water and sink only
    slowly. The Leviathan comes up after you: its charges and ice shards
    aim at whatever height you're at.
  - Fixed a crash when looking at the walls of the Leviathan's lair.
  - THE LEVIATHAN is bigger and more detailed: a longer body that curves
    smoothly, rows of scales and sailed spines, a frill round its head,
    a longer snout full of fangs, long whiskers, webbed flippers and a
    big ribbed tail fin.

PLAYING WITH FRIENDS (up to 4) - NO TAILSCALE, NO ACCOUNTS, FREE
  1. One of you: HOST A GAME, pick or make a world. After a few seconds
     your screen shows a big ONLINE CODE, like  ABCDEF 41234.
  2. Your friends, anywhere in the world: JOIN A GAME, type the code
     (the space can be a space or a dot), press CONNECT.
  Same wifi or hotspot? Even easier: your game shows up in their JOIN
  list by itself - just click JOIN.

  How the code works: the game runs the ssh program that comes with
  Windows 10/11, macOS and Linux, and connects out to Pinggy (pinggy.io),
  a free tunnel service, which hands out the code. Nobody pays, nobody
  signs up. The code changes every time you host. Free Pinggy tunnels
  last an hour; the game gets a new code by itself when one runs out
  (friends already in may need to rejoin with the new code).
  Anyone with the code can join, so only give it to friends.

  If the code doesn't come:
   - Windows: Settings > Apps > Optional features > add "OpenSSH Client"
     (it's on by default on Windows 10 1809 and newer).
   - Some school or work networks block it. Try a phone hotspot.
   - The red line under "NO ONLINE CODE" says what went wrong.

  127.0.0.1 means "this same computer" only. Two games on the SAME
  computer can't both host.
